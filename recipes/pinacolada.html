Pina Colada Recipe

Description

This refreshing, 5-ingredient tropical drink blends smooth and creamy — perfect for hot weather. Allrecipes reviewers praise the easy prep and say it's quicker and better than using a mix.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/2 cup rum
  • 4 tablespoons white sugar
  • 8 cubes ice

Steps

  1. Combine coconut milk, pineapple juice, rum, sugar, and ice in a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.
